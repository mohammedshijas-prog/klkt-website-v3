About Us

Built by CNTXT AI.

KLKT is built by CNTXT AI, a data and AI company in Abu Dhabi. We collect, review and prepare real-world data, and we build AI products on it: Munsit, our Arabic voice AI; AGNTIX, our platform for building AI agents; and KLKT.

AI learns from examples. To understand how everyday work is really done, it needs to see real people doing real tasks, in real workplaces and homes. KLKT is how we gather those examples, with the people who do the work, and pay them for it.

Everyone who takes part agrees in the app, in their own language, before anything is recorded. KLKT only records when they press record, and they see every recording before they send it. Then a person on our team reviews every submission, in our own facilities in Abu Dhabi and Amman. Accepted work is paid. Recordings are never used to identify anyone.

KLKT is part of our CNTXT AI Robotics division. In June 2026, CNTXT AI closed a USD 60 million Series A, co-led by AI71 and BlueFive Capital.
