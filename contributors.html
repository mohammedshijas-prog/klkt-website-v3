How it works

From sign-up to your first accepted task

  1. Get the app and sign up. If your workplace gave you a code, enter it.

  2. Pick a task open in your country. Some start with a short test task, so you know your set-up works.

  3. Do the task the way it asks. For video: your phone on a head strap, sideways, both hands in view, the whole task from start to end. For pages: a clear photo of each page.

  4. Send it in. A person reviews every submission. Accepted work is paid.
